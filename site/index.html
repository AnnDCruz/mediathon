From above, the town looks smaller than it really is.

Prologue — The Roundabout

Nobody remembers when the day actually started.

The palms are the only residents who never leave.

I The Gate

Every town has a way in.

The name is on the wall before you’ve taken three steps in.

By nine, the town has already started moving.

II Market Road

The first stop is never planned.

Market Road doesn’t sell anything you need.

It sells fifteen minutes between one class and the next.

And business is always good.

III The Town Hall

Someone is late.

Someone is always late.

The market is open.

Nobody is buying anything.

IV The Stadium

The match has started.

Once a year, everyone remembers they know how to play.

Now everyone cares about the score.

By noon, nobody remembers why they came outside.

V Gossip Corner

Every town has a step where people stay longer than they planned.

News travels faster when nobody is trying to spread it.

VI The Bazaar Stall

Every stall has a cause, a tablecloth and one very patient volunteer.

Nothing here costs money. Everything costs an afternoon.

VII The Bulletin Board

Some notices are read by everyone.

Most of them are about someone’s missing umbrella.

VIII The Colonnade

There are places in every town where nothing is supposed to happen.

The ivy has been here longer than the timetable.

Even the footsteps are quieter here.

Every town keeps one street for thinking.

IX The Last Bus

By six, the town starts putting itself away.

Conversations become unfinished.

Bags return to shoulders.

The vans are waiting.

Nobody says goodbye properly.

The light is always the last to leave.

From above, it looks exactly the same.

If my university was a small town

Tomorrow, it will start again.
