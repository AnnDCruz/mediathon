# If BPDC Was a Small Town

Link to site: https://anndcruz.github.io/mediathon/

A cinematic, editorial-style photography portfolio website that presents a university campus as a small town through a continuous visual story.

The project uses photography, typography, spacing, and subtle scroll-based interactions to guide the viewer through different parts of the university.

## Project Overview

The experience follows a day-in-the-life journey through the university, using the metaphor of a small town:

- The Roundabout — the town entrance
- The Colonnade — the quiet street
- The Canteen — market road
- The Gathering Place — town hall
- The Court — town stadium
- The Steps — gossip corner
- The Stall — the bazaar
- The Corner Shop — the bulletin-board section
- The Last Bus — the town square
- Tomorrow — the return to the roundabout

The opening and closing are visually connected through the roundabout to create a circular sense of time and place.

## Interactions

The JavaScript provides lightweight interactions including:

- Scroll-based section reveals
- Smooth navigation between sections
- Reading/progress indicator
- Subtle parallax-style visual movement
- Gradual transition into the evening portion of the story
- Responsive navigation behavior

The interactions are deliberately restrained so that photography and narrative remain the primary focus.

## Design Principles

- Photography-first presentation
- Editorial design
- No unnecessary decorative elements
- Responsive on desktop and mobile screens
- Continuous visual journey rather than isolated pages
- Subtle motion instead of excessive animation
- No image cropping or destructive image editing
- Semantic HTML and maintainable CSS/JavaScript
